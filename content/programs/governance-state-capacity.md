This theme examines how Pakistan's institutions, state structures, and governance arrangements respond to the challenges of the twenty-first century. It focuses on democratic development, civil-military relations, public policy, education, institutional reform, and state-society relations, including culture, identity and federalism.

**Key sectors:**
- Democratic governance
- Institutional reform
- Civil-military relations
- Public policy
- Education
- Human capital
- State-society relations
- Culture, identity and national development
- Federal architecture and local governance
