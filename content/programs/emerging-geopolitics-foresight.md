This stream investigates how technological change, particularly artificial intelligence, digital transformation, cybersecurity, biotechnology, and emerging industries is reshaping economies, societies, and the geopolitical environment of states. It combines policy research with scenario-building and long-term forecasting to help policymakers, businesses, and citizens anticipate future challenges and opportunities.

**Key themes:**
- US-China competition
- AI and technological transformation
- Future of global order
- Energy transitions
- South Asia and Central Asia connectivity and change
- Middle East transformations
- Strategic forecasting
- Scenario building
