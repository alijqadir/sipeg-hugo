## Overview

SIPEG's advisory practice translates deep research across governance, geopolitics, and strategic foresight into actionable guidance for decision-makers. We help organisations understand the risks and opportunities in one of the world's most strategically consequential regions.

Our analysis is grounded in three pillars:

{{< icon name="scales" size="1.1rem" >}} **Governance and State Capacity** — Institutional dynamics, regulatory environment, civil-military relations, and political-economy.

{{< icon name="globe-hemisphere-east" size="1.1rem" >}} **China, Asia and Regional Connectivity** — Belt and Road developments, economic corridors, Asian power shifts, and Pakistan's place in the Asian century.

{{< icon name="binoculars" size="1.1rem" >}} **Emerging Geopolitics and Strategic Foresight** — US-China competition, technological disruption, energy transitions, and scenario modelling.

---

{{< icon name="shield-check" size="1.3rem" >}} **Services**

### Political Risk Assessments
Country and sector risk scans tailored to your specific exposure. We assess governance stability, regulatory trajectory, security environments, and geopolitical flashpoints.

### Country Outlook Reports
Forward-looking briefs that synthesise political, economic, and geopolitical developments into a clear trajectory for Pakistan and neighbouring markets. Available quarterly for retainer clients.

### Stakeholder Analysis
Mapping of political, economic, and institutional actors relevant to your engagement. We identify interests, influence networks, friction points, and partnership opportunities.

### Strategic Briefings
Executive-level synthesis delivered to boards, leadership teams, and public agencies. Combines desk research, field intelligence, and scenario modelling.
