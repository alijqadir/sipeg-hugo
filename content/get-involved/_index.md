SIPEG's work is anchored in collaboration. Whether you are a researcher, practitioner, policymaker, or civic leader, there are structured pathways to co-create analysis, co-host events, and shape informed debate on Pakistan's global role.

{{< icon name="newspaper" size="1.3rem" >}} **Contribute Research & Analysis**

- Submit papers, policy briefs, or position pieces aligned with our three focus themes.
- Propose a co-authored publication or working paper.
- Write a guest blog or commentary for SIPEG's weekly series.

→ [Email research enquiries](mailto:info@sipeg.org)

---

{{< icon name="users-three" size="1.3rem" >}} **Join the Fellows Network**

SIPEG's Fellows Network brings together young scholars, doctoral students, practitioners, and diaspora researchers. Fellows contribute analysis, present at dialogues, and gain visibility through SIPEG's platforms.

→ [Apply to join](/apply-fellow/)

---

{{< icon name="chat-centered-dots" size="1.3rem" >}} **Partner on Dialogues & Events**

- Propose a roundtable, webinar, or workshop under the SIPEG Dialogues Series.
- Co-host a session exploring governance, regional connectivity, or strategic foresight.
- Invite SIPEG to brief your institution or team.

→ [Propose a dialogue](/propose-dialogue/)

---

{{< icon name="shield-check" size="1.3rem" >}} **Commission Advisory & Consulting**

- Request a political risk assessment, country outlook report, or stakeholder analysis.
- Engage SIPEG for scenario planning, strategic briefings, or executive education.

→ [Request a consultation](/request-consultation/)

---

{{< icon name="bell" size="1.3rem" >}} **Subscribe & Stay Informed**

- Receive our weekly commentary, monthly policy brief, and quarterly strategic review.
- Get notified about the annual Pakistan Futures Report.

→ [Subscribe to publications](/subscribe/)
