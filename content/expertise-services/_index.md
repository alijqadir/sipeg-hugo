## Our Approach

SIPEG equips policymakers, businesses, scholars, and civic leaders with actionable insight grounded in rigorous research, strategic foresight, and sustained dialogue. Our work spans three integrating themes:

{{< icon name="scales" size="1.1rem" >}} **Governance and State Capacity** — Institutions, democratic development, civil-military relations, public policy, education, human capital, state-society relations, culture, identity, federalism, and local governance.

{{< icon name="globe-hemisphere-east" size="1.1rem" >}} **China, Asia and Regional Connectivity** — China studies, Pakistan-China relations, the Belt and Road Initiative, multipolarity, Asian middle powers, regional integration, economic corridors, technology and development.

{{< icon name="binoculars" size="1.1rem" >}} **Emerging Geopolitics and Strategic Foresight** — US-China competition, AI and technological transformation, the future of global order, energy transitions, South and Central Asia connectivity, Middle East transformations, scenario building, and strategic forecasting.

{{< icon name="newspaper" size="1.3rem" >}} **Publications & Commentary**

Beyond our research papers and blog, SIPEG produces a tiered publications programme to keep stakeholders informed at every tempo:

- **Weekly Commentary / Discussion Paper** — Rapid-response analysis on developments shaping Pakistan and the region.
- **Monthly Policy Brief** — In-depth assessments connecting global and regional trends to Pakistan's strategic choices.
- **Quarterly Strategic Review** — A comprehensive outlook synthesising research, scenario work, and dialogue outcomes across all three themes.
